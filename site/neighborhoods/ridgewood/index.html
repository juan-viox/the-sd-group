ABOUT RIDGEWOOD

The village everyone pictures when they imagine Bergen County.

Ridgewood is the village everyone pictures when they imagine Bergen County: a vibrant, walkable downtown full of restaurants and shops, a Saturday farmers' market, and tree-lined streets of well-kept homes.

The NJ Transit station puts Manhattan within easy reach, which is why Ridgewood stays in demand year after year. Add a nationally recognized school district and you have one of the most sought-after addresses in the region.
