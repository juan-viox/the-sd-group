ABOUT WYCKOFF

Room to breathe, without leaving the conveniences behind.

Wyckoff is a township that rewards families who want room to breathe. Larger lots, mature trees, and classic colonial architecture set the tone, and the public schools are consistently among the most respected in North Jersey.

There's no train in town, so most commuters drive or catch a bus, trading a few minutes for more land and a genuinely residential feel. Boulder Run and the nearby shops handle the everyday, and Ridgewood's downtown is a short drive when you want more.

People & Lifestyle

Life in Wyckoff revolves around family, sports, and the outdoors. Weekends fill up with youth athletics, the Boulder Run and Cedar Hill shops, and time at the James A. McFaul Environmental Center and the town's parks. It's a settled, community-minded place where people tend to put down roots and stay.

Schools

Served by Wyckoff Public Schools (K-8), with high school at Ramapo High School:

  • Coolidge Elementary
  • Lincoln Elementary
  • Sicomac Elementary
  • Washington Elementary
  • Eisenhower Middle School
  • Ramapo High School (Franklin Lakes)
