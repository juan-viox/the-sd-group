ABOUT OAKLAND

Where the county meets the outdoors.

Oakland sits where Bergen County meets the Ramapo foothills, trading sidewalks for more land, trees, and access to the outdoors. The Ramapo River, parks, and nearby trails give it an easygoing, nature-forward feel.

With larger properties and strong value relative to the county, it appeals to buyers who want space and quiet with quick access to Routes 287 and 208 for the commute.

People & Lifestyle

Oakland is for people who want the outdoors close by. The Ramapo River, the historic Van Allen House, and nearby county parks and trails shape daily life, with local shopping along Ramapo Valley Road. It's relaxed, spacious, and easy to reach via Routes 287 and 208.

Schools

Served by Oakland Public Schools (K-8), with high school at Indian Hills:

  • Dogwood Hill School
  • Heights School
  • Manito School
  • Valley Middle School
  • Indian Hills High School (Oakland)
