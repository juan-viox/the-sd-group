ABOUT MIDLAND PARK

A town people are pleasantly surprised by.

Midland Park is a small, welcoming borough that offers some of the best value in the northwest corner of the county. Mid-century ranches and capes line quiet, tree-shaded streets.

It's a town people are often surprised by: calm, affordable relative to its neighbors, and close enough to Wyckoff and Ridgewood to enjoy everything nearby.

People & Lifestyle

Midland Park keeps a small-town rhythm: local shops and eateries, community events, and easy access to the parks and recreation of the surrounding area. It's an unpretentious, friendly town that offers a lot of quality of life for the money.

Schools
