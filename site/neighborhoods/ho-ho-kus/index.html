ABOUT HO-HO-KUS

Under two square miles, and tightly knit.

At under two square miles, Ho-Ho-Kus is one of the smallest and most tightly knit boroughs in Bergen County. A train stop, the historic Ho-Ho-Kus Inn, and a highly rated K-8 school anchor a village where neighbors know each other.

Homes range from historic colonials to newer builds, and the walkable, calm character makes it a favorite for families who want charm without sacrificing the commute.
