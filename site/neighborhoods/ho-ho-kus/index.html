ABOUT HO-HO-KUS

Under two square miles, and tightly knit.

At under two square miles, Ho-Ho-Kus is one of the smallest and most tightly knit boroughs in Bergen County. A train stop, the historic Ho-Ho-Kus Inn, and a highly rated K-8 school anchor a village where neighbors know each other.

Homes range from historic colonials to newer builds, and the walkable, calm character makes it a favorite for families who want charm without sacrificing the commute.

People & Lifestyle

Ho-Ho-Kus lives like a true village: small enough that people know their neighbors, with the historic Ho-Ho-Kus Inn, The Hermitage, and a walkable center at its heart. Community events and a single, well-regarded K-8 school keep the town tight-knit.

Schools

Served by the Ho-Ho-Kus Public School (K-8), with high school at Northern Highlands Regional:

  • Ho-Ho-Kus Public School (K-8)
  • Northern Highlands Regional High School (Allendale)
