ABOUT ALLENDALE

Small-town Bergen County, in the best sense.

Allendale is a picture of small-town Bergen County: a compact downtown, Crestwood Lake for the summers, and a Main Line train station for the commute. Families are drawn by the tight community and the strong schools.

Homes lean toward classic colonials and updated capes on comfortable lots, in a borough that feels friendly, established, and easy to settle into.
