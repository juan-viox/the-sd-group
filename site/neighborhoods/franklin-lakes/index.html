ABOUT FRANKLIN LAKES

Where Bergen County spreads out.

Franklin Lakes is where Bergen County spreads out. Expansive lots, lake communities like Urban Farms and High Mountain, and some of the most private estates in the county define the landscape.

It's a drive-first town built for space and seclusion rather than sidewalks, favored by families and executives who want land, privacy, and top-tier schools within striking distance of the city.

People & Lifestyle

Franklin Lakes is defined by space and privacy. Days center on the outdoors, from High Mountain Park Preserve to the Franklin Lakes Nature Preserve, with private clubs like the Indian Trail Club and the Urban Farms Shopping Center for the essentials. It draws established professionals and families who want room, quiet, and a short reach to Manhattan.

Schools

Served by Franklin Lakes Public Schools (K-8), with high school at Ramapo and Indian Hills:

  • Colonial Road School
  • High Mountain Road School
  • Woodside Avenue School
  • Franklin Avenue Middle School
  • Ramapo High School
  • Indian Hills High School
