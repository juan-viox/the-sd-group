ABOUT GLEN ROCK

A lot packed into a walkable borough.

Glen Rock packs a lot into a compact, walkable borough: two NJ Transit stations, a friendly main street, and strong schools that draw young Bergen families year after year.

It's often the entry point for buyers who want the Ridgewood lifestyle at a slightly gentler price, with an easy commute and a real sense of community.

People & Lifestyle

Glen Rock is walkable and busy in the best way, with two train stations, a compact downtown, and a strong culture of youth sports and community events. It's a connected, active town that appeals to people who want an easy commute and a real neighborhood feel.

Schools

Served by Glen Rock Public Schools:

  • Byrd, Central, Clara E. Coleman & Hamilton Elementary Schools
  • Glen Rock Middle School
  • Glen Rock High School
