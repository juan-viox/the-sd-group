THE STORY

About Sarah

I'm Sarah De Jesus, a Realtor® with Keller Williams Village Square Realty and the founder of The SD Group.

After years of building businesses and raising my family, I chose real estate because it brings together everything I love: homes, design, entrepreneurship, and building meaningful relationships with people.

Purchasing our family's home in Wyckoff inspired me to turn a long-time passion into a career dedicated to helping others find a place to call home. I believe buying or selling a home is about so much more than real estate; it's about the next chapter of your life.

Today, I proudly serve buyers, sellers, and investors throughout Bergen County with thoughtful guidance, honest communication, and a commitment to making every client feel informed, supported, and confident throughout the process.

Whether you're purchasing your first home, selling a property you've loved for years, searching for your forever home, or growing your investment portfolio, my goal is always the same:

To provide an exceptional experience built on trust, professionalism, and genuine care.

I believe success in real estate isn't measured only by the number of homes sold, but by the relationships built along the way. My goal is to become the trusted advisor my clients turn to and confidently recommend for years to come.

Helping people find a home is what I do.

Building relationships that last is why I do it.

Creo que el éxito en bienes raíces no se mide solo por el número de casas vendidas, sino por las relaciones construidas en el camino. Mi meta es ser la asesora de confianza a la que mis clientes acuden y recomiendan con seguridad por años.

Ayudar a las personas a encontrar un hogar es lo que hago.
